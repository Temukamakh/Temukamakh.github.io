A five-day block-lecture course on non-destructive testing and measurement techniques, given in Tbilisi by Dr. Harald Glückler of the Institute of Technology and Engineering (ITE) at Forschungszentrum Jülich.

What the course covered

Four lectures, each on one way of examining a part or a system without damaging it:

  • X-rays in engineering: how X-rays are generated and attenuated, film and digital radiography, computed tomography and neutron radiography.
  • Infrared thermography: the radiation laws and emissivity, infrared cameras, and passive, lock-in and pulse thermography.
  • Ultrasonic testing in material science: sound in solids, reflection and attenuation, probes and A-, B- and C-scans, defect location and thickness measurement.
  • Leak detection: leakage rates, test methods, vacuum gauges and helium leak detectors.

Assessment

The course ended with a written test for everyone who attended. The grade and class percentile have not been announced yet.
